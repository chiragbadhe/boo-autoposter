That “urgent” message asking for your password? It might be bait 🎣👻

Phishing is when criminals send fake emails, texts, DMs or calls to get you to click harmful links or share personal details.
Red flags: panic and urgency, requests for passwords or card details, odd short links, look-alike names like amazan.com.
Don’t click. Check through the real site or app, report it, then delete it.
Turn on MFA so a stolen password alone isn’t enough.
