A notebook that thousands of computers keep a copy of ⛓️👻

A blockchain stores records in blocks, and each block carries the fingerprint (hash) of the one before it.
Change one old block and the chain breaks, so the edit is easy to spot.
New blocks only get added when the network agrees.
It started with Bitcoin in 2008, and it’s also used to track goods and share records.
