Died behind a wall again? Blame the lag 🎮👻

Every move you make travels to a game server and back. That round trip is your ping, measured in milliseconds.
Far-away servers, Wi-Fi, busy networks and lost packets all make it worse.
Games hide some of it: your client predicts your moves, smooths other players between updates, and the server can rewind time to check your shot.
Riot runs Valorant on 128-tick servers, so the game updates 128 times a second.
Quick fixes: plug in a cable, pick the nearest server, pause big downloads.
