Your password is one lock. 2FA adds a second one 🔐👻

Two-factor authentication asks for a second proof after your password.
That proof can be something you have (your phone, a key) or something you are (your fingerprint).
Codes and push taps help a lot, but passkeys and security keys also stop phishing.
Turn it on in your security settings and save your backup codes.
