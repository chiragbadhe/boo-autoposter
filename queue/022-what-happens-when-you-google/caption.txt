Quick quiz: you hit Enter on Google. What happens first? 🌐👻

Commit to your answer, then swipe 👉

Your browser first looks up Google’s IP address using DNS. It checks its own cache, then your computer, router and internet provider.
Next it opens a connection to the server (with a TLS handshake for HTTPS) and sends an HTTP request.
The server sends back a response, and your browser draws the page, fetching images, CSS and JavaScript as it goes.
All of that usually takes well under a second.
