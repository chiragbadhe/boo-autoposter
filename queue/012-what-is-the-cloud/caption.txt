“The cloud” isn’t in the sky. It’s someone else’s computers ☁️👻

Cloud computing means using computing power, storage and apps over the internet, and paying for what you use.
The servers live in big data centers, and the provider looks after them.
You can scale up for busy days and back down after.
IaaS, PaaS and SaaS are three ways to rent it.
