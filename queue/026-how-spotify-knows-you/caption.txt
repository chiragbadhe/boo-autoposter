How does Spotify know your music taste so well? 🎧👻

Spotify says searching, listening, skipping and saving all shape how it reads your taste.
It also looks at what similar listeners enjoy (collaborative filtering), what people write about artists online, and the sound of the tracks themselves.
Your taste and each song become lists of numbers, and songs with close numbers get recommended.
Not everything is a machine: some playlists are still picked by human editors.
