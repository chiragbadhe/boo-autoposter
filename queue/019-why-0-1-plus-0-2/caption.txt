Your computer thinks 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004. It’s not broken 🤯👻

Computers store decimals in binary, and 0.1 never ends in binary.
So it gets rounded to fit 64 bits, and the tiny errors add up.
For money, count in whole paise or use a decimal type.
