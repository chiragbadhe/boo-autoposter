Arrays or linked lists? 🧱🔗 Boo explains in 45 seconds 👻

📦 Array: items sit side by side, so jumping to any index is instant, O(1). Inserting in the middle means shifting everything after it, O(n).
🔗 Linked list: each node points to the next. Finding an item means walking the links, O(n), but once you're there, inserting is just changing two arrows, O(1).

Fast lookups → array. Lots of inserts → linked list.
