Can WhatsApp read your chats? Nope, and here’s why 🔐👻

WhatsApp uses end-to-end encryption, built on the Signal Protocol. It’s on by default.
Your message is locked on your phone, travels through WhatsApp’s servers scrambled, and only your friend’s phone can unlock it.
WhatsApp says the keys change with every message, so a later key can’t open older ones.
Want proof? Open a contact’s Encryption screen and compare the 60-digit security code or scan the QR together.
Two honest catches: chat backups are only end-to-end encrypted if you turn that on, and WhatsApp can still see metadata like your phone number, IP address and when you use the app.
