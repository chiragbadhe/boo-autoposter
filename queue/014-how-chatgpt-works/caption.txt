A chatbot doesn’t think up answers. It predicts them, one word at a time 🤖👻

It learns patterns from huge amounts of text by guessing the next word, over and over.
When you ask something, it picks a likely next word, adds it, and repeats.
A bit of randomness means the same question can get different answers.
It can sound sure and still be wrong, so check important facts.
