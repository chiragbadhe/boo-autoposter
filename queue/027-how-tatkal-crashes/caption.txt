Why does IRCTC slow down at Tatkal time? A, B, C or D? Comment how you did 👇🚆

AC Tatkal booking opens at 10 AM and non-AC at 11 AM, one day before the journey.
So lakhs of people hit the same page, for the same small pool of seats, in the same minute.
The Railways told Parliament in 2013 that hits crossed 20 lakh at Tatkal time, against a normal 6–7 lakh, and the whole system was strained.
Systems designers call this a traffic spike: the hard part isn't the average day, it's the busiest minute.
