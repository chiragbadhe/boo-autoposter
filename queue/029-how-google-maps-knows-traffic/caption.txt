How does Google Maps know the road ahead is jammed? 🚗👻

When people navigate with Google Maps, their pooled (aggregate) location data shows how fast traffic is moving on each road right now.
Maps also remembers how each road usually behaves at each hour, like a highway that crawls every evening.
Machine learning, built with DeepMind, mixes live and past data to guess what traffic will look like 20 or 50 minutes into your trip, and reroutes you if a jam is coming.
It also uses closures and incidents reported by users, plus speed limits and road data from local governments.
In 2020 Google said its ETAs were accurate for over 97% of trips.
