Hackers don’t sit there guessing your password. A script does it for them 🔓👻

Brute force tries every combination, so short passwords fall fast.
Dictionary attacks try the most common passwords first.
Credential stuffing reuses logins leaked from other sites, so one reused password can unlock many accounts.

Your defence: long, unique passwords from a password manager, plus 2FA.
