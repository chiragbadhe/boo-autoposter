You scan, type a PIN, and the chai is paid. What actually happens in those few seconds? 💸👻

UPI is a network run by NPCI that sits between all the banks.
Your app sends the request through its bank, NPCI routes it, your bank checks your PIN and balance.
Then your bank debits you and their bank credits them.
