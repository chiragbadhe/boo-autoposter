How does Linktree actually work? 🔗👻

Your bio only has room for one link. Linktree turns that one link into a menu of all your links: videos, socials, shop, newsletter.

1️⃣ Make a page and pick a username
2️⃣ Add your links
3️⃣ Put your Linktree link in your bio
4️⃣ Fans tap, pick, and go
