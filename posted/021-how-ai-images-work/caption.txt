Ever wondered how AI turns a sentence into a picture? 🎨👻

Most image generators, like Stable Diffusion, are diffusion models.
In training they watch real images get covered in noise and learn to predict that noise.
To make a new image, they start from pure random static and remove noise step by step.
Your prompt is turned into numbers by a text encoder, and it guides every clean-up step.
