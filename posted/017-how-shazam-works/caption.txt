5 seconds of a song in a noisy café and Shazam just knows. How? 🎧👻

Shazam turns sound into a spectrogram and keeps only the loudest peaks.
Pairs of peaks become tiny hashes, a fingerprint of the clip.
Those hashes are looked up in a huge table, and the song where they line up in time wins.
