Why does Netflix rarely buffer, even on hostel Wi-Fi? 🍿👻

Netflix built its own content delivery network, called Open Connect (started in 2011).
It gives qualifying internet providers its servers, called Open Connect Appliances, at no charge.
Shows are copied to these boxes during off-peak hours, using popularity models to pick what goes where.
So when you press play, the video usually comes from a server inside or near your own internet provider.
