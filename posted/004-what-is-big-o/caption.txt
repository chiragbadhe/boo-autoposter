Ever heard a coder say “that’s O(n)” and nodded along? 😅👻

Big O tells you how an algorithm’s work grows as the input grows.
O(1) stays flat, O(log n) halves its way through, O(n) checks everything once, O(n²) compares every pair.
Finding 1 name in 1,000: up to 1,000 checks one by one, or about 10 with binary search.
